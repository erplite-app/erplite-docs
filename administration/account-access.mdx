title: Account Access
description: "Sign in to the right ERPLite workspace and recover workspace access."
---

ERPLite workspaces have their own workspace URL. This keeps users scoped to the right account, even when the same email address belongs to more than one workspace.

## Find Your Workspace

Go to the main ERPLite sign-in page and choose one of these options:

| Option | When to use it |
|--------|----------------|
| **Workspace URL** | You know your workspace name, such as `your-company.erplite.app`. |
| **Find by email** | You do not remember which workspaces your email belongs to. |

When using **Find by email**, ERPLite sends workspace links to the email address if it belongs to any workspaces. The page does not display the workspace list directly.

## Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace URL">
    Use the workspace URL shared by your administrator, or enter your workspace name from the workspace finder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Sign in with Google if enabled for your account, or use email/user ID and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete verification if prompted">
    If your account has two-factor verification enabled, enter the one-time code sent to your email or phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    After successful sign-in, ERPLite opens the Tables area by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shared Email Across Workspaces

The same email address can belong to multiple ERPLite workspaces. Always sign in from the correct workspace URL so your login is scoped to the intended account.

<Tip>
If a user says they signed in but cannot see expected tables or records, first confirm they are on the correct workspace URL.
</Tip>

## SSO and SCIM

Some companies use SAML SSO for centralized identity management. In SSO-enforced workspaces:

- Users sign in through the company's identity provider.
- Password and Google login may be blocked.
- New users can be provisioned automatically based on the configured identity provider.
- Deprovisioned users can be revoked so they lose access quickly.

Contact your ERPLite administrator if your SSO login fails or your role/team assignment looks incorrect after signing in.

## Account Recovery Checklist

1. Confirm the workspace URL.
2. Confirm the email or user ID belongs to that workspace.
3. Ask an admin to verify that the user is active.
4. If two-factor verification is enabled, check that the user's email or phone number is current.
5. For SSO workspaces, confirm the user is active in the identity provider.

## AI Guidance

### When to Use This Feature

An AI should use Account Access guidance when users cannot sign in, cannot find the correct workspace, or belong to multiple ERPLite accounts.

### How to Use It

1. Confirm the workspace URL.
2. Confirm the user's active status.
3. Confirm whether password, Google, two-factor, or SSO is the expected login method.
4. Use workspace recovery by email if the workspace is unknown.

### How to Verify

The AI should verify that the user lands in the correct workspace and can access the expected home page, tables, or tasks.
