Use **Settings > Account** for workspace-level details that affect every user.

## General Settings

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|------------------|
| **Account ID** | The unique ID for this workspace. You may need it when coordinating with ERPLite support. |
| **Workspace URL** | The subdomain users should use to sign in to this account. |
| **Account Name** | The business name displayed for the workspace. |
| **Default Timezone** | The timezone used by schedules, cron automations, reports, and date/time displays that rely on account settings. |

<Tip>
Set the default timezone before configuring scheduled automations. This keeps daily digests, reminders, and operational cutoffs aligned with your business day.
</Tip>

## Slack Integration

Connect Slack when your automations should send messages to channels or users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Click **Add to Slack** and approve the requested workspace permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Slack in automations">
    Configure Send Slack Message actions in automations after the account shows as connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Slack is disconnected, automations that send Slack messages stop working until it is reconnected.

## When to Update Account Settings

- Your company name changes.
- Users are signing in through a new workspace URL.
- Scheduled automations are running at the wrong local time.
- You are rolling out Slack notifications or changing Slack workspace access.

## Best Practices

- Keep the account timezone aligned to the primary operations timezone.
- Copy the Account ID only when needed for support or integration setup.
- Test one Slack automation after connecting or reconnecting Slack.

## AI Guidance

### When to Use This Feature

An AI should use Account Settings when timezone, workspace identity, account name, Slack connection, or support coordination is part of setup.

### How to Use It

1. Confirm account name and workspace URL.
2. Set the default timezone before scheduled automations are created.
3. Connect Slack before configuring Slack notifications.
4. Save changes and test the affected behavior.

### How to Verify

The AI should verify that scheduled actions use the intended timezone and Slack automations can post successfully when Slack is connected.
