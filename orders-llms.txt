# ERPLite Orders Module — API Reference for LLMs

> Sell goods and ship them. Customers, sales orders, dispatches, stock claims,
> coverage, and the seam into Inventory. Served by the BFF at
> `/bff-service/orders`. Every request is account-scoped by the bearer token.

## Access

- Pilot module behind an ACCOUNT ALLOWLIST. An account not on it gets
  `403 not_enabled` on EVERY route including `/health` and `/whoami`.
- Installing and enabling are different. A fully installed account still answers
  `not_enabled` until it is added to the allowlist.
- Capabilities come from ROLE BINDINGS, not the platform role. An account with no
  bindings grants nobody anything.
- Roles form a ladder: viewer -> operator -> supervisor -> admin. Highest wins,
  cumulative.
- A machine principal is NOT on the ladder and CANNOT be escalated by a binding.
  Only three named principals hold anything:
    orders-intake       view, order.intake, order.acknowledge, order.receipt_confirm
    production-adapter  view, demand.write_back
    external-fulfilment view, dispatch.allocate, dispatch.ship
  A generic api user holds NOTHING.

NOTE: `export` is floored at OPERATOR, not viewer (settled to match Purchase).

## Core model

- A customer order is NEVER refused for want of stock. Stock is checked only at
  ALLOCATION (gate 3).
- Quantities live in an engine POSITION keyed by `line_uid`. The columns on
  `ord_order_line` are a PROJECTION. When they disagree the engine is right.
- The coarse status is AUTHORISED, never derived. Seven values:
  Draft, Received, Acknowledged, In_Progress, Delivered, Closed, Cancelled.
  You never write one; you call an action. `GET /orders/:orderNo` returns
  `ceiling` and `transitions[]` with a reason for each refusal.
- How far it has been MADE and how far it has GONE are two SEPARATE DERIVED
  fields, recomputed from the line quantities on every write:
  `production_status` (Not_Started / Partially_Produced / Fully_Produced) and
  `dispatch_status` (Not_Dispatched / Partially_Dispatched / Fully_Dispatched).
  Never write these. To ask "has it shipped", read `dispatch_status` — the
  coarse status cannot answer it, because an order can be part made and part
  sent at once.
- In_Production, Ready and Dispatched were coarse statuses until 2026-08-10 and
  no longer exist. If you see them in older data or code, they map to
  In_Progress plus the two dimensions above.
- Quantities are ABSOLUTE, never deltas. `allocated_qty: 100` means "is now 100".
  Retry = no-op. `0` releases.
- allocated_qty and dispatched_qty are BUCKETS. Shipping moves units from the
  first to the second; after a full ship allocated_qty is 0.
- Orders writes NO `inv_*` table; nothing outside Orders writes an `ord_*` table.
- required = ordered - cancelled + returned
  pending_production = max(0, required - produced)
  pending_dispatch   = max(0, required - dispatched - allocated)
  open demand        = required - dispatched   (allocated is still owed)

## Reservation policies

none      (default) no claim taken; a competing allocation succeeds; the second
          SHIPMENT is refused by the negative-stock floor at posting
advisory  claim recorded and warned about; Inventory does NOT defend it; every
          screen must label the number advisory
enforced  NOT AVAILABLE. The policy write refuses it and names the three missing
          Inventory prerequisites. It does NOT silently downgrade to advisory.

A claim is DERIVED from the allocation entry, recomputed on every position write,
so it cannot outlive what it was held for. Reducing to zero RELEASES it.
NEVER sum advisory and enforced claims — the response has no field for a total
on purpose.

## Governance policy keys (PUT /policies/:key, admin, reason MANDATORY)

locked_fields_after_ack, cancel_after_ship (allow|refuse, default allow),

acknowledgement_required

Seeded by the installer, then owned by the account — a re-install leaves a
changed row alone rather than reverting it.

subscribes yet. `orders.dispatch.allocated` and `orders.claim.changed` carry the
ACTOR and the PREVIOUS quantity — the entry keeps only the after, so the pair is
the audit trail.

## Things that trip people up

- `delivery_warehouse` is where goods come FROM (the demand warehouse), not the
  delivery address. The ship-to is the destination.
- A ship-to is only valid for the customer it was created under.
- `comms_preference` is REQUIRED on a customer and has NO DEFAULT.
- The customer status field is `status` ("active"/"inactive"), not a boolean.
- Ship-to fields are `address_line_1`, `address_line_2`, `postal_code`.
- Export paths are SINGULAR: /exports/order, /exports/dispatch.
- `dispatch_date` is OVERWRITTEN at ship time with the shipping date.
- Coverage `unknown` is NOT a shortfall of zero; render it "could not tell".
- An unavailable feasibility component has NO qty field — it cannot read as 0.
- Reconcilers REPORT by default; pass ?commit=true to write.
- The idempotency key is never a lock; concurrency control is the ship-attempt
  index race. Inventory's `applied: false` means "already posted, carry on".

## Not in v1

Returns/credit notes/refunds; invoices and receivables; credit limit as a gate
(shown, never enforced); a system-computed delivery date; automatic allocation;
notifications of any kind; multi-currency; any direct write to a stock table.
