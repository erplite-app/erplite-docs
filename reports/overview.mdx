Reports give managers and teams a structured way to review operational performance. ERPLite supports native reports and embedded analytics dashboards.

## Report Types

| Report Type | Use It For |
|-------------|------------|
| **Native report** | ERPLite-rendered reports such as stockout risk or spec-driven operations reports. |
| **Embedded analytics** | Metabase dashboards and cards embedded inside ERPLite. |
| **Spec-driven report** | A configured report layout with tabs, date ranges, cards, and tables. |

## Common Report Needs

- Stockout risk and recommended actions.
- Operations summary by date range.
- Sales order status and delays.
- Production progress.
- Purchase order and GRN status.
- Inventory movement and reorder planning.
- Work order completion and overdue work.

## AI Guidance

### When to Use This Feature

An AI should create or update reports when the business asks for management review, trend analysis, exception monitoring, or recurring operational summaries.

Use Home dashboard widgets for daily action. Use Reports for deeper analysis and review.

### How to Use It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the business question">
    Example: "Which SKUs are at stockout risk?" or "Which sales orders are late?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify source tables">
    Choose the tables and fields needed for the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose report type">
    Use native/spec reports for ERPLite-rendered reports and embedded analytics for existing dashboards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add filters">
    Include date range, status, team, customer, vendor, or category filters where needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and test">
    Open the report, adjust filters, and compare results with underlying records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to Verify

The AI should verify:

- The report opens from the Reports list.
- Date-range filters change the results.
- Tabs, cards, and tables show data from the correct sources.
- Names display clearly instead of raw relation IDs.
- Results match the filtered records in the source tables.
- Report access matches the intended user audience.

## Report Review Checklist

- What decision should this report support?
- Who reviews it?
- How often is it used?
- Which records should be included?
- Which statuses or exceptions should be excluded?
- What action should a user take after seeing it?
